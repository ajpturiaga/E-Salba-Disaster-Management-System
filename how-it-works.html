HOW IT WORKS

Simple steps. Faster response.

E-Salba simplifies the disaster response process by connecting people who need help with people who can provide it.

RESPONSE PROCESS

From emergency to response.

01
STEP ONE

Submit an Emergency Request

A person in need submits an emergency request containing their location, emergency type, contact information, and situation details.

02
STEP TWO

Emergency Alert

The emergency request is received by the system and becomes available to authorized administrators and volunteers.

03
STEP THREE

Volunteer Assignment

An available volunteer can be assigned to the emergency case based on the situation and response requirements.

04
STEP FOUR

Response and Assistance

The volunteer responds to the emergency and provides the required assistance.

05
STEP FIVE

Case Resolution

Once the emergency is addressed, the case can be updated and marked as resolved.

Organized disaster response starts with better communication.
